The editor preview is not the deliverable. Your saved file may use a different background, crop or size from the document you were looking at. A short export routine catches those differences before you share the wrong result.

## Decide what the file is for

A reusable sticker needs transparency around its subject. A finished card usually needs an intentional background. A complete composition may include both opaque and partly transparent areas. Write that choice down before changing export options.

![A workflow from editable source to PNG export to a reopened light-and-dark test.](/guides/export-flow.svg)

For a transparent sticker, hide background and test layers. For a finished image, leave the intended background visible. A checkerboard in the editor should not be recreated as an actual patterned layer.

## Inspect size and boundaries

Check both the document dimensions and the visible subject. Large transparent margins can conceal a small, low-detail cutout. Crop unnecessary outer space while preserving soft shadows and fine protruding details.

If the design includes text, inspect at the final display size. A name that is legible at 200% zoom may be unreadable in a small chat preview. If necessary, shorten the message or increase the text size rather than exporting an unnecessarily huge canvas.

## Export a clearly named copy

Choose PNG when you need alpha transparency. Keep the editable original separately. A name such as `birthday-maya-transparent-v2.png` tells you more than `final-final-new.png`, especially after several experiments.

Do not rename a JPEG extension to PNG and expect transparency to return. Conversion changes the container; it does not reconstruct pixels already flattened onto white.

## Reopen outside the editor

Check the saved file's dimensions and inspect it on light and dark backgrounds. Confirm that the canvas has not clipped a letter or a shadow. Then test the exact transfer route you intend to use: download, file sharing, or clipboard.

If the result changes only after transfer, retain your exported master and troubleshoot the receiving app. Repeatedly re-exporting the source will not fix an app that treats pasted images as opaque photos.

## Keep two useful versions

For a design you expect to reuse, keep a transparent PNG and an editable source. If you also need a flattened sharing image, save it under a distinct name.
