A travel diary works better as a short sequence than as three nearly identical views. Choose one photograph that establishes the place, one that shows a small detail, and one that closes the moment. The sequence supplies the story; stickers can connect the pages.

## Assign a role to each image

Use a wide view for the opening, a close-up such as a shell or café table for the second image, and a quieter photograph for the last. You do not need an impressive destination. The contrast between scales creates interest.

![Three small portrait pages sharing one accent colour and a repeated caption position.](/guides/sequence.svg)

Crop each image for its own subject instead of forcing all three to have the same horizon height. Consistency should come from the design system, not from sacrificing the photographs.

## Repeat one small feature

Choose a single motif, such as a shell, star or paper label. Repeat it in the same general position across the sequence. Vary its size only if there is a clear reason, such as leaving space for a face or sign.

The [beach-day pack](/packs/beach-day/) is one starting point for a coastal diary. You can also use a plain paper scrap so the theme works for a city walk, museum visit or train journey.

## Keep captions concrete

Write a short observation for each page: what you noticed, ate or kept. A sentence about a specific detail is more useful than adding several unrelated decorative slogans.

Keep the caption position and type treatment consistent. If the photo beneath that area is busy, use the same quiet panel on all three pages rather than inventing a new solution each time.

## Check continuity and privacy

View the sequence in order at phone size. The opening should establish context, the middle should add something, and the last should feel like a stopping point. Remove a page if it merely repeats the previous one.

Check photographs for tickets, addresses, identification documents and bystanders before sharing. A decorative crop may expose details that were less noticeable in the full photograph.

Save all three exports with numbered filenames so they remain in order. The [file-organisation guide](/blog/organize-sticker-library/) gives a simple naming scheme, and the [consistent-series exercise](/blog/consistent-story-series/) helps when you want to extend the diary without making every page identical.
