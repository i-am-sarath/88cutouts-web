A thank-you card becomes personal through the words you choose. The design should make that note easy to read, with decoration supporting the greeting rather than replacing it.

## Choose a compact format

For a digital exercise, use a square canvas and divide it into three regions: a short decorative heading, the personal message, and a small sign-off. Keep generous margins so the card feels intentional when viewed as a small image.

![A card layout with a large heading, plain-text note and a smaller sign-off.](/guides/type-hierarchy.svg)

Write the note before choosing stickers. `Thank you for helping me move` gives the design a clearer purpose than a generic greeting followed by a large empty region.

## Use expressive letters once

Make `THANK YOU` with the cut-out letters in the [scrapbook maker](/scrapbook/?tool=text). Put the personal note in a conventional font below it. Using decorative letters for every sentence creates unnecessary decoding work.

Choose a line break that keeps the heading balanced without shrinking it. The [line-break guide](/blog/cutout-text-line-breaks/) explains how to compare alternatives by meaning and visible width.

## Add one meaningful accent

A flower, heart or paper scrap can sit beside the heading or sign-off. Choose the object because it fits the recipient or occasion, not because you need to fill the last corner.

Keep decoration outside the note's reading area. If a long ribbon tail touches the final word, move the bow rather than reducing the note's size.

## Prepare the sharing version

For a complete digital card, export with the intended background visible. A transparent card can appear differently in each chat app, especially if the lettering was designed for a light surface.

Open the saved image at normal phone size and read every word. Send the message as ordinary text too if it contains information the recipient may want to copy or search.

If you want a printed copy, start from the intended physical size and check the usable pixel dimensions of the assets. The [print-size calculation](/blog/print-size-for-png-stickers/) explains why changing a resolution label alone does not add detail. Keep the card personal; business or client uses require checking the [asset licence](/license/).
