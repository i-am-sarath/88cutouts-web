A palmier biscuit, which is naturally heart-shaped, photographed so the caramelised layers and the sugar glaze read clearly. Warm golden-brown throughout with no bright highlight, which makes it one of the easier food cutouts to place — it does not fight the photo underneath. The layered spiral gives it internal detail that holds up at medium size.
