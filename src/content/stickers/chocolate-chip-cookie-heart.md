A heart-shaped chocolate chip cookie, photographed so the chips and the crumb texture read clearly against the golden bake. The heart outline is slightly irregular, like a real cut biscuit rather than a stamped shape. Warm-toned and high-contrast, so it holds up on most backgrounds. An easy pairing with the coffee cups, and a better Valentine's element than a plain heart because it does not take itself seriously.
