A red and white electric guitar shown full length at a slight angle, with the fretboard, pickups and scratchplate all clearly rendered. Tall and narrow — one of the few cutouts here genuinely suited to running down a vertical edge of a Story. The length is also its weakness: shrunk to fit a corner, the body and neck merge into a red line.
