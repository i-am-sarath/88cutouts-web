A pair of thin wire-framed round spectacles with pale lenses, finished with a white die-cut border. Almost entirely outline, so the photo shows through the lenses, which makes it one of the few cutouts you can lay over a face and have it look deliberate. The frames are thin lines and they thin further as you scale down — do not shrink this one far.
