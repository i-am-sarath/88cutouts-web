A five-pointed star drawn as a thick hand-painted outline in two tones of pink, with a visible brush texture and a second star traced inside the first. Deliberately wonky rather than geometric. Because it is an outline rather than a filled shape, the photo shows through the middle, which makes it far easier to layer over a face or a subject than a solid star would be.
