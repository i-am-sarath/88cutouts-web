A black record with a sleeve image of two hands almost touching across the label, echoing the Sistine Chapel. As with the cassette, it sits on a pale square backing that is opaque rather than transparent, so expect a light panel behind it on a dark photo. The image on the label makes it a subject rather than a generic record.
