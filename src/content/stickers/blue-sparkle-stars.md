Four four-pointed sparkles in a strong cobalt blue, drawn with a rough dry-brush edge rather than clean vector points, and grouped at different sizes and angles. The irregular grouping is what stops it reading as a graphic overlay. Because the blue is saturated and mid-toned, it holds up against both pale and dark photos. Good as the last thing you add to a frame that is nearly finished.
