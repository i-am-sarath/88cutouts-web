A lily rendered in translucent glass, catching pink, peach and pale blue across the petals so the colour shifts from one edge to the other. The whole appeal is the iridescence, which needs both size and a background with some tonal variation behind it — on a flat white photo it washes out to nearly nothing. One of the few cutouts here that genuinely improves on a darker image.
