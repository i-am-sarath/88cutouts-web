A large sun with long wavy rays in molten gold, the disc textured like hammered metal. Considerably bigger and more detailed than the two sun charms, and meant to be used at size rather than tucked away — the ray detail is the appeal and it is the first thing lost when scaled down. Top corner is the natural home, since that is where the eye expects light.
