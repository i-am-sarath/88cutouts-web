A tall clear cup of iced coffee with a domed lid and a straw, photographed side-on so the layers of coffee and melting ice are visible through the plastic. Dark and high-contrast against most photos. Tall and narrow, which makes it one of the few cutouts here that suits a vertical edge of a Story rather than a corner.
