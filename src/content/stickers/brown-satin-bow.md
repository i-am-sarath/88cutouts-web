A chocolate-brown satin bow, symmetrical, with a bright highlight running along the top of each loop. The sheen is what makes it read as satin rather than paper, and highlights need room, so keep it above about a third of the frame width. The deep warm brown is unusually easy to place: it sits on cream, white and dark photos alike without clashing.
