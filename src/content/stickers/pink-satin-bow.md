A bright pink satin bow, smooth and symmetrical, with a soft highlight across the top of each loop and two short tails below. Simpler and more saturated than the mauve version, with less visible fabric texture. The strong colour means it reads at small sizes where a paler bow would vanish, which makes it the most corner-friendly bow in the set.
