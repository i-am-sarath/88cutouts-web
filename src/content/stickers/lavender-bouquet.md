A tied bunch of lavender stems, painted rather than photographed, with a cream wrap at the base and a white die-cut border around the whole thing. Tall and narrow, so it suits a vertical edge. The purple holds up against both light and dark photos, which makes this one of the more reliable flower cutouts when you are not sure what it is going on.
