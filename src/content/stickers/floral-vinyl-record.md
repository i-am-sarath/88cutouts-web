A black vinyl record with cream roses overlaid across the grooves, sitting on a pale square backing. Worth knowing before you use it: the square backing is opaque, so this one pastes with a visible light panel behind the record rather than as a clean circular cutout. On a white or cream photo that is invisible; on a dark one the panel will show.
