A soft yellow and cream gingham bow with long tails and a fine check running through the fabric. The palest and quietest of the gingham bows — closer to the beige one than the red or pink — which makes it a layering piece rather than a focal point. It will disappear on a bright photo, so give it something darker to sit against.
