A lipstick kiss print in deep burgundy, with the texture of the lip lines left in rather than smoothed out. Small, dark and instantly legible, and one of the few marks here that reads as something left on the photo rather than placed on it. Works at an angle far more than square to the frame — a kiss print is never level.
