A heart dusted in cocoa powder onto a pale biscuit base, so the shape has a soft, slightly powdery edge rather than a crisp one. Small and warm-toned. The soft edge means it blends into warm photos rather than sitting on top of them, which is useful when you want the heart present but quiet — the opposite job to the glossy red heart.
