A single plumeria bloom in cream and butter yellow, the five petals overlapping in a slight pinwheel with the yellow deepening toward the centre. Painted rather than photographed. It is large and pale, which means it needs a mid-tone or darker photo behind it — on a bright background the cream petals lose their edges entirely.
