A five-pointed star in rust-red felt with a visible stitched edge, made to look like an embroidered patch rather than a flat shape. The stitching is fine detail that disappears first, so this rewards being placed a little larger than you would place a plain star. It pairs obviously with the tan version of the same patch — two at different sizes reads better than two the same.
