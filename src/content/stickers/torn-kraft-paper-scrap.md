A piece of kraft paper torn down one side so the edge is ragged and the fibres show, with faint printed texture across the surface. It is a base layer, not a subject — put lettering, a bow or a charm on top of it and rotate it a few degrees so it stops reading as a rectangle. Large and roughly square.
