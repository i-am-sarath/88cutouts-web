Torn Kraft Paper Scrap is a transparent PNG sticker with a hand-cut, paper-collage edge, so it drops onto a Story without a white box around it. It's built to look like a scrap torn from an actual sheet, with torn paper, kraft, scrap and texture — useful as a background layer under other stickers. It's small enough to sit near an edge without crowding whatever photo it's placed over.
