A watercolour bloom in peach and cream, the petals fading almost to white at their edges with a small gold-green centre. The palest flower in the set, and the softest — the outer petals genuinely disappear against a white or very light photo, so it needs a mid-tone or darker background to hold its shape. Large and roughly square.
