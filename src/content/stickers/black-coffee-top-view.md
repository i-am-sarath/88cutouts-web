A cup of black coffee shot straight down, so you get the full circle of the crema with its bubble pattern and the handle jutting out to one side. The dark disc gives you a strong shape with real internal texture, and the offset handle stops it reading as a plain circle. Works on a morning Story without a caption, and the flat top-down angle sits well over a table photo shot the same way.
