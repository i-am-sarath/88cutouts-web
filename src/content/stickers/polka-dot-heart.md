A deep red heart covered in evenly spaced white dots, flat and illustrated with no shading at all. The dots give it internal texture without fine detail, so unlike the glossy or crumpled hearts it holds together when shrunk. Flat colour also means it sits into a photo rather than floating above it. One of the more forgiving hearts here.
