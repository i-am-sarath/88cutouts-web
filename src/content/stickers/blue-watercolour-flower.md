A six-petalled watercolour bloom in pale blue with a soft green-yellow centre, the pigment pooling darker at the petal edges the way real watercolour does. It is nearly square and quite large, so it works as a subject in its own right rather than an accent. Blue recedes against most photos, which makes this one a good choice when you want a flower present without it taking over the frame.
