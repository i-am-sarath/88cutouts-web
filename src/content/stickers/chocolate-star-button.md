A five-pointed star moulded in milk chocolate with four sewing-button holes punched through the middle, so it reads as a button and a sweet at the same time. The holes give it internal detail that keeps it interesting at medium size, though they close up if you shrink it too far. Matt rather than glossy, which makes it easier to place on a bright photo than the glossy pieces here.
