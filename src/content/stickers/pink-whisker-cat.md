A cat's face with long exaggerated pink whiskers drawn out either side and a small bow on its head, cut out with a white sticker border. The drawn whiskers are the distinguishing feature and they extend the shape sideways well beyond the face, so allow for that width when you place it — they clip badly against the edge of a frame.
