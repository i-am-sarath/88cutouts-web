A silver compact camera decorated with charms and a small bow, with a blank white screen on the back. The blank screen is worth knowing about: it reads as an empty panel rather than transparency, so this is a cutout to place over a photo, not one to drop a picture into. The metallic body needs a mid-tone background to show its shape.
