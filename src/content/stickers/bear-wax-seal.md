A round bronze wax seal stamped with a bear's face, complete with the irregular poured edge that a real seal leaves. It is small, circular and high-contrast, which makes it one of the more versatile things here — it works as a full stop at the end of a layout, or overlapping the corner of a photo as though it is holding it down. Pairs naturally with the paper scraps and the envelopes.
