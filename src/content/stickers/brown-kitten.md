A dark chocolate-brown kitten photographed front-on with its paws resting over an invisible edge, as though it is peering over something. That pose is the useful part — line the bottom of the cutout up with the top of a caption box or the edge of a photo and it looks like it is leaning on it. Very dark overall, so it needs a light background to keep its outline.
