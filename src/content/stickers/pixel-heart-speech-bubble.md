A pixel speech bubble with a hard drop shadow and a red pixel heart inside it, all in stepped black outline. The bubble shape means it reads as something being said rather than something placed, which makes it one of the few cutouts that works pointing at a person in a photo. Keep the tail aimed at whoever it belongs to.
