A classic sitting teddy in mid-brown plush, front-on and symmetrical, with the fur texture clearly visible. Symmetry makes it forgiving: it looks right centred, cornered or tilted. It is one of the warmer, quieter cutouts here, which means it works as a supporting element next to something with more contrast rather than as the thing you build a frame around.
