A five-pointed star in deep red felt with a white stitched outline running just inside the edge, made to look like a sewn patch. Compact, symmetrical and high-contrast. The white stitching does double duty as an outline, which is why this reads more clearly on a dark photo than the brown and tan patches in the same family.
