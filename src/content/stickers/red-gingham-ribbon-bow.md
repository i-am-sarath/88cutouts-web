A narrower red gingham bow tied from a thin ribbon, with long tails trailing well below the knot and wired edges that keep the loops crisp. Where the other gingham bow is a fabric mass, this one is a line — better for running along an edge or trailing off the corner of a photo than for sitting as a block of pattern.
