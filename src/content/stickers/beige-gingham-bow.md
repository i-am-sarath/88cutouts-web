A soft beige gingham bow with four loose tails and a fabric texture you can actually see the weave in. The check is small, so this is one to place larger than feels necessary — shrunk into a corner the pattern compresses into flat noise. The neutral colour is its advantage: it sits on warm and cool photos alike without needing anything else in the frame to match it.
